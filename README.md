# Money Maintainer

A personal finance management platform for tracking **budgets, expenses, investments, portfolios, and assets** in one place.

## 🎯 Purpose

Money Maintainer is being built as a centralized system to understand where money goes, plan where it should go, track investments, and maintain a complete view of personal financial assets and obligations.

The goal is to move beyond a simple expense tracker and provide a structured **personal financial management system**.

## 💰 Core Modules

### 1. Budgeting

Plan and manage money across different periods and categories.

- Monthly and yearly budgets
- Category-based budget limits
- Planned vs actual spending
- Budget utilization
- Remaining budget
- Recurring budget items
- Budget summaries and trends

### 2. Expense Tracking

Record and analyze day-to-day financial transactions.

- Personal expenses
- Recurring expenses
- Category and sub-category tracking
- Payment method tracking
- Cash, bank account, and credit card transactions
- Expense notes and references
- Necessary / Useful / Optional classification
- Monthly and yearly expense analysis
- Spending trends

### 3. Portfolio Management

Track investments and understand portfolio performance.

- Stocks
- Mutual funds
- ETFs
- Fixed deposits
- Other investment instruments
- Holdings
- Buy / sell transactions
- Investment cost
- Current value
- Profit / loss
- Portfolio allocation
- Investment performance

### 4. Asset Management

Maintain a complete record of owned assets.

- Vehicles
- Property
- Electronics
- Gold
- Bank balances
- Investments
- Other valuable assets
- Purchase price
- Current estimated value
- Purchase date
- Asset depreciation/appreciation
- Asset history

### 5. Liabilities & Credit

Track money owed and financial obligations.

- Credit cards
- Loans
- EMIs
- Borrowed money
- Money lent to others
- Outstanding balances
- Repayment tracking
- Interest and repayment information

### 6. Financial Overview

Provide a consolidated view of personal finances.

- Total assets
- Total liabilities
- Net worth
- Monthly income
- Monthly expenses
- Savings
- Investments
- Portfolio value
- Cash flow
- Budget status

## 🧩 Financial Structure

Money Maintainer is designed around a clear separation between:

**Income → Budget → Expenses → Savings → Investments → Assets**

while also tracking:

**Liabilities → Credit → Loans → Repayments**

This allows the system to represent the complete financial picture rather than only recording expenses.
